# Project Context

## Repository
https://github.com/TheProjectACE/ProjectACE.git

## Deployment
Not deployed yet. The site is a static page and can be hosted on GitHub Pages or imported into Vercel.

## Product
ProjectACE is a youth-led research and innovation community spanning six fields: aerospace, water science, space science, robotics, land and environmental research, and creative design.

## Audience
School and university students, young independent researchers, mentors, and potential collaborators.

## Goal
Introduce ProjectACE, make the six departments easy to explore, explain the research mindset, and encourage interested visitors to find a department that fits them.

## Brand
ProjectACE. Brand line: “Curiosity into creation.” Voice is curious, clear, capable, welcoming, and youth-led.

## Visual Direction
Modern high-tech and futuristic. Deep navy surfaces, electric-cyan highlights, restrained blue and violet, strong Space Grotesk-style headings, crisp line icons, and orbital geometric artwork. Responsive layouts with restrained motion and clear contrast.

## Page Structure
1. Sticky navigation
2. Hero with primary “Explore our departments” CTA and secondary “How we work” CTA
3. Three guiding principles
4. Six department descriptions: AeroACE, AquaACE, AstroACE, RoboACE, LandACE, CanvasACE
5. Research approach: Question, Investigate, Build and share
6. Invitation to explore a department
7. Footer navigation

## Copy Direction
Use direct, concrete language. Explain what each department explores. Do not invent membership counts, achievements, active projects, partnerships, contact details, or launch dates.

## Assets
Single self-contained `index.html`; CSS and minimal JavaScript are embedded. Department and brand icons are inline SVG geometry. Google Fonts are an optional external font dependency with local fallbacks. No image assets required.

## Functionality
Responsive navigation with accessible mobile menu, internal section links, hover and keyboard-focus states, reduced-motion support, and responsive department layouts. Static landing page; no backend, contact form, or membership submission is connected.

## Avoid
Frameworks, package managers, fabricated claims, placeholder contact links, excessive animations, generic stats, emoji used as the icon system, and unnecessary dependencies.

## Current Decisions
- The organization combines youth research, student engineering, collaboration, and innovation.
- Audience includes school and university students, with mentors and collaborators also welcome.
- Main CTA: Explore our departments. Secondary CTA: How we work / Get involved.
- Visual direction: modern, attractive, tech-inspired, and futuristic.
- The user has no visual reference site; establish a distinct visual identity from the brief.
