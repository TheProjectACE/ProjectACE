# ProjectACE Research Content Standards

## Content labels

Use these project statuses: Proposed, In Progress, Testing, Published, Archived. A status describes the current stage, not the quality or credibility of a result.

Add a clear demonstration label to sample or instructional content that could be mistaken for research.

## Evidence

For each result, preserve the research question, method, equipment or dataset, units, observation date, and limitations. Link claims to sources that support them. Never invent a source, observation, publication, or contributor.

## Simulated data

Identify generated values and educational visualizations as simulated. State model assumptions and explain why a model should not be used for real-world safety-critical predictions.

## People and consent

Do not display a contributor's name, image, or role publicly without authorization. Do not collect applications until the site has a real endpoint and a clear privacy notice.

## Water and environmental research

Basic visual checks do not establish that water is potable. Impact claims need a baseline, a repeatable measurement method, and appropriate caveats.
