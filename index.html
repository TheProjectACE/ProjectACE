How we think

Make the question worth chasing.

Good research starts with a question. Progress comes from testing it, learning from the result, and improving the next attempt.

01

Question

Notice a problem, challenge an assumption, or follow an idea that you cannot stop thinking about.

02

Investigate

Read, observe, sketch, simulate, and design a test that can teach you something useful.

03

Build and share

Turn what you learn into a prototype, a clear explanation, or a better question for the team.
